Not connecting?
  1. Open nRF Connect and connect to the Knog Scout.
  2. While still connected, tap the menu and choose Refresh services. It does nothing once disconnected.
  3. Disconnect there, come back here, tap Connect.

Pairing must be done in Android Settings › Bluetooth — a web page cannot show the PIN dialog.

Event log

Arming writes 01 to the Scout's Control Point characteristic and disarming writes 02; state comes from Alarm Active notifications. Link-only by default. Connect opens the Bluetooth link and stops there — no service discovery, which is the step this Scout breaks. While linked, the button on the Scout arms and disarms it. Enable app controls additionally runs discovery to get Arm/Disarm buttons here; if that fails the link survives and the device button keeps working. Chrome or Edge on Android only; Firefox and iOS have no Web Bluetooth. Unofficial tool, not affiliated with Knog.
